Dora Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-12
Last updated: 2026-03-12

This Privacy Policy explains how Dora ("Dora", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Dora mobile application and related services (the "Service").

If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and authentication data provided through Supabase Auth.
  • Profile information: username, display name, avatar URL, and bio.
  • User content: trips, places, routes, notes, media metadata, and export requests.
  • Support communications: messages or emails sent to support.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device type, OS version, app version, language, and basic diagnostics.
  • Log and usage data: API access logs, feature usage, request/response timing, and error events.
  • Approximate location-derived data from content you create (for example, trip place coordinates), when you choose to provide it.

1.3 Media and file data

  • Photos/media you upload are stored in configured storage services.
  • Export artifacts (for example rendered videos) may be stored temporarily or persistently depending on product settings and your actions.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • provide and operate the Service;
  • authenticate users and secure accounts;
  • sync user data across devices;
  • generate exports and downloadable/shareable artifacts;
  • troubleshoot, monitor, and improve reliability;
  • prevent abuse, fraud, and policy violations;
  • communicate critical product and policy updates;
  • comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal Basis (where applicable)

Depending on your location, our legal basis may include:

  • performance of a contract (providing the Service),
  • legitimate interests (security, diagnostics, improvement),
  • consent (where required), and
  • compliance with legal obligations.

4. Sharing and Third-Party Processors

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with service providers that process data on our behalf, including:

  • Supabase (authentication, database, storage),
  • Railway (application hosting),
  • AWS (video rendering/export infrastructure),
  • Mapbox (maps and geospatial services),
  • Firebase (analytics/remote config, when enabled),
  • Sentry (error monitoring),
  • Foursquare (place search/metadata, when used).

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

5. Ads and Subscriptions

Dora may offer ad-supported experiences and paid subscriptions (for example premium features).

If ads are enabled, ad partners may process limited technical data as permitted by platform policies and law.

6. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to operate the Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Typical retention behavior:

  • account/profile and core trip data: retained until account deletion request is completed;
  • logs and diagnostics: retained for limited operational/security windows;
  • backups: may persist for a limited period before rotation.
