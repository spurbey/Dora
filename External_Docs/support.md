Need help with Dora? Contact us:

- Email: `sumitpurbey101@gmail.com`
- Subject line suggestion: `Dora Support - <issue>`

Please include:
- app version,
- device model and OS version,
- steps to reproduce,
- screenshots or screen recordings (if possible),
- your account email (if support is account-specific).

## Typical Response Window

- Initial response target: within 3 business days.
- Critical account/access issues: prioritized.

## Common Requests

- login issues,
- export failures,
- share link issues,
- account deletion requests,
- billing/subscription questions.
