Dora Account Deletion

Effective date: 2026-03-12
Last updated: 2026-04-24

You can request permanent account deletion in either of these ways.

Option 1: In-app (recommended)

  1. Open Dora.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion.

Option 2: Email request

Send a request to: sumitpurbey101@gmail.com
Use subject: Dora Account Deletion Request
Include the email tied to your Dora account.

What deletion does

When completed, deletion removes your Dora account and associated app data, including profile, owned trip data, and associated media metadata (including audio-linked capture records where applicable), subject to legal/security exceptions.

Deleting your Dora account does not delete your Google account (or other third-party provider account).

Processing timeline

  • In-app confirmed deletions are processed immediately when possible.
  • Email requests are processed after identity verification, usually within 3 business days.
  • Some systems (for example backups) may take additional time to fully rotate out deleted data.

Exceptions

Some records may be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate compliance obligations.
